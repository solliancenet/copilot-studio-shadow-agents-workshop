# Task 00-01 — Get your lab access

## Introduction

You cannot review what you cannot see. There are two ways through this lab: in the live Halcourt Mutual
demo tenant, where you gather the estate's data yourself into blank CSVs, or offline from the filled-in
**artifact pack** if you can't reach the tenant. The tenant is the preferred path. This task sets you up
for whichever one you use.

## Description

If you can reach the tenant, you will sign in with the lab account the facilitator hands out, open the
**Power Platform admin center** (PPAC) and confirm you can see the environments list, including your own
lab environment, learn the rule for when to change anything, then download the blank CSVs you fill in as you work. If you can't, you will
download the filled-in artifact pack and work from it offline. The tenant is the preferred path and the richer hands-on
experience; the pack covers every task.

One tenant can hold several copies of the Halcourt Mutual estate. The names in your copy carry your
**estate id**, and the default environment may also hold other estates' agents.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] *(Live tenant only)* You can sign in to the shared Halcourt Mutual demo tenant using the lab account the facilitator handed out.
- [ ] *(Live tenant only)* The Power Platform admin center opens, you can see the **Environments** list, and you can point to your own lab environment in it.
- [ ] *(Live tenant only)* You can say where you may change things (your own lab environment) and where you may not (the shared estate).
- [ ] You have downloaded the artifact pack from the Estate Binder: the blank CSVs if you signed in, the filled-in set if you can't reach the tenant.
- [ ] All five CSVs open cleanly: `agents-export.csv`, `connections-export.csv`, `agent-identities-export.csv`, `users-export.csv`, `environments-export.csv`.

## Key Tasks

### 01: Get into the demo tenant and open the Power Platform admin center

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Expand this section for detailed steps</summary>

1. Get your **lab account** from the facilitator: a username and password for an account in the lab tenant, along with the tenant's domain and your **estate id**.

   Where a lab page shows a plain name, look for:

   - **Accounts:** `marcus.webb.<estate_id>@<domain>` and *Marcus Webb (&lt;estate_id&gt;)* for *Marcus Webb*.
   - **Environments, groups, SharePoint sites, the DLP policy:** `Claims-Test-<estate_id>`, `All-Employees-<estate_id>`, `HR-Benefits-<estate_id>`, `Halcourt-Prod-DLP-<estate_id>`.
   - **Agents in the default environment:** *Expense Helper (&lt;estate_id&gt;)* and *Onboarding Guide (&lt;estate_id&gt;)*.
   - **Your own lab environment:** `Lab-<estate_id>-NN`, where `NN` is the number on your handout.
2. Open a private browser window, so your own work account doesn't sign you in to the wrong tenant, and go to the **Power Platform admin center** at `https://admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com`. Sign in with your lab account.
3. In the left rail, choose **Manage**, then **Environments**. Confirm you can see the environments list,
   and find **your own lab environment** (`Lab-<estate_id>-NN`) in it.
4. Learn the rule of engagement: look, don't change. Your lab account has the access the tasks need to read
   the estate, and you don't change the estate: it is shared by everyone in the room, and one person's change
   becomes everyone's result. In Exercise 05, your facilitator applies each control, once, for the whole room,
   and you retest it. The one place you may change anything yourself is your own lab environment: you are its
   administrator, so you can always open a security role there and read its scope. In the shared estate that
   depends on your lab account having a licence. Nobody else's work is in your environment.

### 02: Download the artifact pack and open the five CSVs

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Expand this section for detailed steps</summary>

1. Open the [Estate Binder]({% link worksheets/index.md %}) and find **The artifact pack** section.
2. If you signed in to the tenant, download the five blank CSVs: [`agents-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/blank/agents-export.csv %}), [`connections-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/blank/connections-export.csv %}), [`agent-identities-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/blank/agent-identities-export.csv %}), [`users-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/blank/users-export.csv %}), and [`environments-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/blank/environments-export.csv %}). You fill them in from the portal and APIs as the lab goes on.
3. If you can't reach the tenant, download the five filled-in CSVs instead: [`agents-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/agents-export.csv %}), [`connections-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/connections-export.csv %}), [`agent-identities-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/agent-identities-export.csv %}), [`users-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/users-export.csv %}), and [`environments-export.csv`]({% link worksheets/artifact-pack/environments-export.csv %}).
4. Open each CSV in a spreadsheet tool (or a text editor). A blank CSV shows only its header row; a filled-in one also shows its data rows. No need to analyze anything yet.
5. Note the columns that join the files: `agents-export.csv` names an `environment` and an `owner_upn`, `environments-export.csv` lists environments, and `users-export.csv` lists UPNs. You will join across them in Exercise 02.

</details>

{: .note }
> The live tenant is the preferred path: working in the portal and APIs is the richer, hands-on way

## Summary

If you signed in, you have a seat in the Halcourt Mutual tenant, the preferred way through the lab, and
the blank CSVs you fill in from it. If you couldn't, you have the filled-in set. Either way, you are ready
to start asking how many agents there are, who owns them, and what they can reach. Next, get oriented to
the org and the day.
