# Task 02-04 — Produce the Agent Estate Inventory

## Introduction

This is the checkpoint for Exercise 02. Your inventory from Task 02-02 and your Flags table from Task
02-03 make up your working **Agent Estate Inventory**: every agent, who is accountable for it, and which
agents are flagged and why. What's left is to total the flags and check your work. It's the map the
rest of the day works from; a hole here becomes a blind spot in every later exercise.

## Description

You will total your Flags table into the counts the
[01 — Agent Estate Inventory]({% link worksheets/templates/01-agent-estate-inventory.md %}) template asks
for, sanity-check them against your inventory, and save the finished deliverable.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] The inventory table is complete — one row per agent, every cell holding a value, `Not applicable`, or `Unknown — follow up`, all four environments represented.
- [ ] Every flagged agent you cleared carries a one-line justification (from Task 02-03).
- [ ] The counts are filled: agents found, environments covered, agents flagged Orphaned or Unknown / ambiguous owner, agents flagged Shared connection.
- [ ] Environments covered equals **four** — you can name them, and you did not silently drop the sandbox or default environment.
- [ ] The finished inventory is saved to your Estate Binder.
- [ ] You compared your Flags table and counts with the suggested ones, and can explain any difference.

## Key Tasks

### 01: Compute the counts and finalize

The counts summarize your Flags table. Fill them, then check your work before you move on.

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Expand this section for detailed steps</summary>

1. Fill the **Count** section of the template: agents found; environments covered; agents flagged
   Orphaned or Unknown / ambiguous owner; agents flagged Shared connection. Each count is the number of
   agents with a yes in those columns of your Flags table. An agent with a yes in both columns counts
   once.
2. **Sanity-check environments covered = 4.** Your inventory's **Environment** column should name all
   four: `Halcourt (default)`, `Halcourt-Prod`, `Claims-Test` and `Maker-Sandbox`. If one is missing, an
   agent is missing too: find it in your `agents-export.csv`, or in the Power Platform admin center's
   **Manage** → **Inventory**, and add its row.
3. **Sanity-check the counts against your inventory.** Every agent in your "Orphaned or Unknown /
   ambiguous owner" count should trace to its **Owner (status)** (left, disabled or a shared mailbox) or
   to a **Tool calls** owner who has left. Every agent in your "Shared connection" count should trace to a
   **Tool calls** owner who isn't the agent's owner, is a service account, or has left or is disabled. If
   the numbers don't line up, one of your hunts is wrong.
4. **Confirm no flagged agent is unjustified.** Every agent your hunts flagged has a line from Task
   02-03: confirmed or cleared, and why.
5. **Save the finished inventory to your Estate Binder.** It is the input to Exercise 03, where you turn "what
   exists" into "what can it actually reach, and as whom."

</details>

## Summary

You built the working inventory: one row per agent across every environment, a Flags table with a
decision for each agent, and a reason for every flagged agent you cleared or confirmed. It tells you what
exists and who is accountable for it, and which agents to look at first.

It doesn't tell you how much those agents can reach. You know Claims Copilot runs as `svc-claims`, but not
what that account can read; you know Policy Lookup Bot sends an API key, but not what the API returns.
Those Unknowns are what
[Exercise 03]({% link docs/03_identity_and_effective_access/03_identity_and_effective_access.md %})
measures.
