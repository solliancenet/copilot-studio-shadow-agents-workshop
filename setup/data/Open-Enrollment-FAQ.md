# Meridian Mutual — 2026 Open Enrollment FAQ

**When is open enrollment?** November 1–21, 2026. Changes take effect January 1, 2027.

**What plans are available?** Medical (three tiers), dental, vision, an HSA/FSA option, and supplemental
life. See the Benefits site for the full plan comparison.

**How do I add a dependent?** Complete the life-event form within 30 days of the qualifying event.

**Who do I contact?** The HR Benefits team via the Benefits site.
