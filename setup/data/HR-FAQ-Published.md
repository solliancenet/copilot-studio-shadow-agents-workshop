# Halcourt Mutual — HR Frequently Asked Questions

*Published for all employees. Last reviewed for the 2026 plan year.*

**When is open enrollment?** November 1–21, 2026. Elections take effect January 1, 2027.

**What plans are available?** Medical (three tiers), dental, vision, an HSA/FSA option, and supplemental
life. The full plan comparison is on the Benefits site.

**How do I add or remove a dependent?** Complete the life-event form within 30 days of the qualifying
event (marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage).

**How much paid time off do I get?** Full-time employees accrue PTO monthly and may carry over up to five
days into the next year. Check your balance in the HR portal.

**What are the standard working hours and holidays?** Core hours are 9:00–17:00 local time. Halcourt
observes ten company holidays; the schedule is posted each January.

**How do I request a leave of absence?** Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least 30 days in
advance when the leave is foreseeable, and contact the HR Benefits team for medical or family leave.

**How do I update my address or emergency contact?** Update your profile in the HR portal; changes sync to
payroll overnight.

**Who do I contact for help?** The HR Benefits team, via the Benefits site or the HR portal.
