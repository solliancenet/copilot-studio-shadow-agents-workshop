## Coverage D — Comprehensive (Other Than Collision)

Comprehensive covers direct and accidental loss to the insured vehicle from perils **other than
collision**, subject to a **$250 deductible**. Covered comprehensive perils include, without
limitation:

- **Hail, windstorm, and other weather events**
- Fire and lightning
- Theft and vandalism
- Falling objects
- Glass breakage
- Contact with an animal (e.g., a deer strike)

A **hail** loss is adjudicated as a **Coverage D comprehensive** claim, not a Coverage B collision
claim. The $250 comprehensive deductible applies per occurrence.

## Coverage B — Collision

Collision covers direct and accidental loss caused by the vehicle's impact with another vehicle or
object (including a single-vehicle upset), subject to a **$500 deductible** per occurrence.

## Notes

- Rental reimbursement (Coverage C) is included and available on covered Coverage B and Coverage D
  losses while the insured vehicle is in an approved repair facility.
- Deductibles apply per occurrence and are netted from any loss settlement.
