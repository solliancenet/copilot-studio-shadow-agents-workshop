# Halcourt Mutual — Claims Handling Guide (Adjuster Reference)

This guide gives claims staff a consistent triage process. It does **not** authorize a coverage
decision; every claim is settled by a licensed human adjuster.

## §1 Intake and triage

1. Confirm the claim number, the policy it is filed under, and policy **status** (must be Active on
   the date of loss).
2. Confirm **premium standing**. A returned or missing premium payment near the date of loss is a
   standing flag that must be noted for the adjuster — it does not by itself deny coverage.
3. Retrieve the policyholder of record and prior claims on the same policy.

## §2 Anomaly notes

Flag, for human attention, any of the following:

- More than one open claim on a policy within a 90-day window.
- A claim amount that is large relative to the vehicle's actual cash value.
- A returned (bounced) premium payment in the billing period preceding the date of loss.
- A mismatch between the described peril and the coverage being claimed.

## §3 Coverage assessment by peril

### §3.1 Comprehensive claims

A loss from a **non-collision peril** — **hail**, windstorm, fire, theft, vandalism, falling
objects, glass, or animal contact — is assessed under **Coverage D — Comprehensive** and carries the
**$250 comprehensive deductible** (see Declarations p.2). Confirm the described cause of loss maps to
a listed comprehensive peril before routing as comprehensive.

### §3.2 Collision claims

A loss from **impact with another vehicle or object**, including parking-lot and single-vehicle
impacts, is assessed under **Coverage B — Collision** and carries the **$500 collision deductible**.
Rear-end, bumper, and tailgate impact damage is a collision peril.

## §4 Recommended next steps (triage output)

Every triage briefing routed to an adjuster should list: coverage line and deductible that appear to
apply, any standing or anomaly flags, and the documents still needed (estimate, photos, police or
incident report). The agent prepares the briefing; the **human adjuster makes the decision**.
